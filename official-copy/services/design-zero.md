## 8. Where Are You Starting From?

**Issue 1 — The Marketing Site and App Don't Match**

Scenario — The company has a polished marketing site and a functional app, but the two look like they belong to different brands — different type scales, different component styles, different color applications.

Outcome — Users who convert from the marketing site arrive in a product that feels unfamiliar, eroding the trust that the marketing site just spent money building. Over time, this disconnect becomes a quiet churn signal that never gets traced back to its real cause.

My Solution — The Brand Audit maps every channel and identifies every divergence. The Master Figma File creates a single source of truth that governs both surfaces. Tokens are synced into Webflow and the GitHub site so future updates propagate across the system automatically.

---

**Issue 2 — The Team Built on Templates and Never Thought About Coherence**

Scenario — Products were assembled component by component, shaped by sprint deadlines and bespoke client requests, without ever stepping back to ask what the system should be.

Outcome — There is no system to maintain, update, or hand off. Every new designer has to reverse-engineer what exists. Every new sprint risks adding more inconsistency on top of what's already there. The technical debt compounds quietly until it's a crisis.

My Solution — Starting from the Brand Audit, I document exactly what exists across every channel, identify what can be unified, and build the system from foundations up — logo, favicon, colors, typography, primitives, tokens — before a single component is built. The result is a system that explains itself.

---

**Issue 3 — The Brand Was Never Instilled Into the Product**

Scenario — The brand exists as a PDF, a mood board, or a Figma file nobody has opened in months. The product was built by engineers using whatever looked reasonable. The marketing site has one visual identity. The app has another. Neither feels intentional.

Outcome — The product never communicates that it belongs to the company. For enterprise buyers, this is a red flag. For end users, it's a signal that the product is unfinished. For the internal team, it means every design decision restarts from zero because there's no shared reference.

My Solution — The Agentic AI deliverable is what makes this different from a standard design system engagement. Once the system is built and documented in `.md` files, your Founders and Product Team can query their own system conversationally — asking what the correct button style is for a destructive action, requesting a component spec that follows your token structure, or checking whether a proposed screen aligns with your design principles. The system becomes a living, queryable reference that the whole team uses without needing to find the designer.

## 11. FAQ

**Is this service for my marketing site only, or does it cover my app too?**
It depends on the package you select. Package 1 covers marketing sites only. Package 2 covers a marketing site plus one web or mobile app. Package 3 covers a marketing site plus multiple products. The Discovery Call is where we confirm which scope fits your situation.

**My company uses WordPress, not Webflow. Does that disqualify me?**
Not at all — it means a Platform Adjustment Add-on applies. Supported alternatives are WordPress with Elementor, Wix, Framer, and Shopify. Any platform outside this list requires a separate discussion during the Discovery Call. Webflow is the default because the design system page and token sync are most stable there.

**We already have a brand guide. Do we still need this service?**
Yes — if that brand guide hasn't been instilled into your product, the problem this service solves is still present. Most brand guides are made for marketing. Design System Zero takes what exists and builds it into the actual surfaces your users interact with, including the AI layer.

**How long does this take?**
Phase 3 — the Master Build — is the longest phase and varies the most: 1 month for a relatively clean, small product and up to 6 months for a complex multi-product company with significant existing UI debt. Phases 4, 5, and 6 add 4–7 additional weeks on top. The Discovery Call is where we scope the timeline accurately for your situation.

**What do you need from us to get started?**
Access to your existing Figma files, your marketing website, your app's staging environment (including non-public screens), and any admin-facing UI. If you have brand guidelines — even rough ones — share those too. The more context upfront, the sharper the audit.

**What if we're mid-sprint and things are actively changing?**
That's normal. The Brand Audit is a snapshot of the current state. If changes are happening during the engagement, I flag them and incorporate them into the system rather than treating them as blockers. The system is designed to accommodate ongoing work — that's the point.

**How do the `.md` files work exactly?**
The `.md` files are structured Markdown documents covering every layer of your design system — foundations, components, writing guidelines, accessibility standards, and decision rationale. They're formatted to be uploaded into AI agents like Claude Projects, Cursor, ChatGPT Projects, or any compatible tool. Once uploaded, your Founder or Product Team can ask questions about the design system and get answers grounded in your own rules — not generic AI output.

**Do we need to have AI tools set up already?**
No. If AI models don't exist yet, I set them up during Phase 6. If you already have a model in place, I refine it using the completed `.md` files. The team tests the output before the final payment is released — you don't pay for Phase 6 until you've confirmed the AI is returning accurate, useful answers.

**What's the difference between the Webflow design system page and the GitHub internal site?**
The Webflow page is for your marketing website's dev team — it lives inside your existing Webflow project and gives them production-ready components to copy-paste directly. The GitHub site is a separate, developer-facing documentation site for your app — it hosts live component code that dev team members can access and copy without opening Figma. Package 1 includes only the Webflow page. Packages 2 and 3 include both.

**What happens if we're not happy with the AI output during Phase 6 verification?**
That's why the verification gate exists. If the AI's outputs don't match the design system accurately, I adjust the `.md` files before the final payment is released. The engagement doesn't close until the team confirms the AI is working correctly.

**What's not included?**
Brand revamp, writing or rebuilding KB content from scratch, and UX design of new product screens. These are separate services. Design System Zero documents and systematizes what exists — it doesn't redesign it.

**Is payment refundable?**
Payments already made are non-refundable for work completed up to that point. If you need to pause the engagement, we can discuss a hold period. If the engagement is cancelled mid-project, deliverables completed to that point are yours to keep.
