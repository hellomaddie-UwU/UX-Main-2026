**Heuristic UX Audit and PRD Write-Up**

## 2. Summary

Most products don't fail because of missing features — they fail because the experience quietly works against the people using it. I conduct expert heuristic UX audits for web apps and websites, combining established design principles with calibrated design judgment to surface the friction points your team hasn't been able to name yet. Every engagement ends with a ranked findings report, structured PRD tickets delivered directly into your workspace, and a sequenced roadmap your team can execute from immediately — no translation work required. Whether you're a SaaS founder watching churn climb, a DTC brand spending on the wrong fix, or an agency that needs a structured discovery process to stand behind, this audit gives your team the clarity and direction to move with confidence.

### For ICP 2 — E-Commerce / DTC Brand
- Conversion rate is a direct revenue lever — and spending on ads or redesigns without diagnosing the leak amplifies the loss rather than fixing it
- Bigger DTC competitors with dedicated design and dev budgets are continuously iterating on their UX — the experience quality gap compounds over time
- Seasonal and category context matters: investing in CRO at the wrong time for your product category burns budget with no return, regardless of how good the UX recommendations are
- Without behavioral analytics in place, there's no internal starting point — the audit replaces the missing data layer with expert observation

### For ICP 3 — Digital Agency
- AI tools are commoditizing surface-level UX feedback — clients are starting to question the value of paying for a review when they can prompt ChatGPT
- The differentiator is structured expert judgment: calibrated design taste backed by real project experience, not a checklist any tool can run
- A named, repeatable audit methodology protects the agency's discovery fees, reduces scope creep, and gives the agency something concrete to sell at the pitch stage

## 9. How It Works

### Website Audit — Process
*2–3 weeks from onboarding to final delivery, depending on product complexity and scope.*

---

**Phase 01 — Discovery**
*Duration: 1 session, ~30–60 minutes*

What happens: We get on a call to determine if this engagement is the right fit. I ask about your website, your team's current situation, and any known pain points. No work begins until both sides confirm it's a match.

Milestone: Fit confirmed, engagement formally begins.

Client Responsibility: Show up with honest context about where the product stands. No prep required.

End of Phase: Signed agreement and onboarding scheduled.

---

**Phase 02 — Onboarding**
*Duration: 1 session, ~45–60 minutes*

What happens: I walk you through exactly what's going to happen, in what order, and what I need from you to do my job properly. We align on scope, user journeys to audit, access requirements, and any technical limitations I should know about upfront.

Milestone: Scope confirmed, access granted, audit journeys defined.

Client Responsibility: Provide access to the staging environment and flag any technical constraints or off-limits areas.

End of Phase: Aligned scope document. **First invoice issued (50%). Audit begins once payment is confirmed.**

---

**Phase 03 — Product Familiarization**
*Duration: 1–5 business days depending on site complexity and available materials*

What happens: Before the audit begins, I spend time getting properly acquainted with the website. This includes hands-on exploration and a thorough review of any documentation, copy, or supporting materials the client provides.

Milestone: Sufficient familiarity confirmed. Audit ready to begin.

Client Responsibility: Provide any documentation, copy guidelines, or context that helps me understand the website accurately. More context upfront means a sharper audit.

End of Phase: Audit compilation begins.

---

**Phase 04 — Audit Compilation**
*Duration: 1–2 weeks depending on scope*

What happens: I conduct the full heuristic audit across the agreed user journeys. Every finding is documented in the Master Figma File with annotated screenshots, a critique legend, and flow-level coverage. Competitor and market analysis are completed in parallel.

Milestone: Master Figma File completed and ready for review.

Client Responsibility: Stay available for any clarifying questions. No heavy lifting required during this phase.

End of Phase: Master Figma File delivered to the designated representative for review and approval.

---

**Phase 05 — Findings Review**
*Duration: 3–5 business days*

What happens: Your designated representative reviews the Master Figma File and filters out findings that are already in progress or not a priority. The curated list is escalated to the founder or primary stakeholder for final sign-off. Once approved, the Issues Rank and Business Impact Report is produced.

Milestone: Founder or stakeholder approves the curated findings list.

Client Responsibility: Ensure the founder or stakeholder is available for final approval within the review window.

End of Phase: Issues Rank and Business Impact Report delivered.

---

**Phase 06 — PRD Write-Up**
*Duration: 3–5 business days depending on ticket volume*

What happens: I write structured PRD tickets for every approved finding and deliver them directly into your team's workspace — ClickUp, Notion, GitHub Issues, or your preferred tool. Each ticket is self-contained and ready to act on.

Milestone: All approved findings converted into executable tickets.

Client Responsibility: Confirm workspace access and preferred ticket format before this phase begins.

End of Phase: Full PRD ticket backlog live in your workspace. Offboarding document issued. **Final invoice issued (50%). Engagement closed once payment is confirmed.**

---

## 11. FAQ

### Website Audit — FAQ

**Fit and Scope**

**Is this service for my marketing site or landing page?**
Yes. The website audit covers any publicly accessible website — marketing sites, landing pages, campaign pages, or content-driven sites. If it lives in a browser and has a user journey, it qualifies.

**How do I know if my website is ready for an audit?**
If your website is live, it's ready. You don't need analytics data or a design team in place. The audit works from what exists today.

**Do you audit mobile apps or web apps?**
Not under this tier. The website audit is scoped for marketing websites and similar properties. If you have a web app, see the Web App Audit below.

**What if we're already updating parts of the site?**
Your representative filters out findings that are already in progress during the Master Figma File review — so you only move forward on findings that are actually new and actionable.

**What does "heuristic" mean in practice?**
It means the audit is grounded in established UX principles — frameworks like Nielsen's 10 heuristics — used as the foundation for every finding. What makes this audit different from a checklist review is that principles provide the structure, but calibrated design judgment catches what a rubric alone would miss: pattern recognition, visual hierarchy instincts, interaction feel, and copy-UX alignment that only come from doing real product work.

---

**Expected Outcomes and Success Measurement**

**What does success look like at the end of this engagement?**
You walk away with a complete audit, a ranked findings report your stakeholder has approved, and PRD tickets live in your workspace ready to act on. Success is your team knowing exactly what to fix and why — without a roadmap needed, because the fixes are self-contained.

**Will the audit guarantee improved conversion?**
The audit surfaces the friction — what you do with the findings determines the outcome. The audit doesn't promise results; it removes the guesswork that was blocking them.

**How many findings can I expect?**
It depends entirely on the site. There's no artificial cap or minimum — findings are whatever the audit surfaces. Every finding in the final report is something your stakeholder has already reviewed and approved as worth acting on.
