## 2. Summary

Most websites and apps weren't designed — they were assembled. Pages were built by developers following rough briefs, copy was written by stakeholders trying to force a point, and nobody with UX judgment was in the room when it mattered most. UX, Rewritten fixes the experience from the journey up — every flow reconceptualized, every word written to move your users forward, and every screen built on a Stylescape direction your team has already approved. Whether your product lives on the web or in an app, the result is an experience that feels intentional, converts with less friction, and carries the human judgment that no AI tool can replicate.

## 8. Where Are You Starting From?

---

**Issue 1 — The product was built without a proper user journey**

Scenario — The website or app was assembled by a developer or from a template with no UX process behind it — screens exist but no one designed the flow between them.

Outcome — Users drop off at predictable points because the journey was never intentionally designed to move them forward.

My Solution — I conceptualize proper user journey flows from the ground up, stacking fixes in chronological order so the experience is rebuilt with intention rather than patched screen by screen.

---

**Issue 2 — Stakeholders are writing the experience instead of letting it breathe**

Scenario — Decision-makers push for more text on every screen to force a point, overriding the layout and leaving no room for users to process information and make purchasing decisions on their own.

Outcome — The page becomes a wall of copy that overwhelms rather than converts — users disengage before they ever reach the action the stakeholder was trying to drive.

My Solution — I push for concise copy that drives purchasing decisions forward by empathizing directly with your target audience.

---

**Issue 3 — The team wants a new coat of paint when the foundation needs fixing**

Scenario — Leadership knows the product looks outdated or underperforming, but defaults to requesting UI changes — new colors, new typography, a visual refresh — rather than addressing the underlying UX problems driving the numbers.

Outcome — The product looks slightly better but behaves exactly the same. Drop-off points remain untouched, conversion stays flat, and the visual refresh becomes the reason the next round of fixes gets delayed.

My Solution — I organize and collect flows and products from a bigger picture, then isolate and reframe each gap to push your target users to their desired outcome.

**Phase 1 — Discovery**
*Duration: 1 session, 30 to 60 minutes*

*Duration: 1 session, 45 to 60 minutes*

What happens: I walk you through the full process, timeline, and everything I need from you to do the job properly. We align on scope, access requirements, and confirm your designated contacts. Stylescape direction preferences are discussed here — the more context you provide, the stronger the options I can present.

Client Responsibility: Share any existing brand assets, references, or visual preferences. Confirm your designated contacts.

End of Phase: Aligned scope document. **First invoice issued (50%). Work begins once payment is confirmed.**

*Duration: 3 to 5 business days*

What happens: Before any design begins, I study your product independently. This includes reviewing your existing website, reading through your KB and marketing materials, and researching your USP and market position. I come in as a real user would — without assumptions baked in from client briefing alone.

Milestone: Sufficient product familiarity confirmed. Stylescape work begins.

Client Responsibility: No action required during this phase. The more materials shared during onboarding, the sharper this phase runs.

End of Phase: Stylescape production begins.

**Phase 4 — Stylescape Submission and Approval**
*Duration: 3 to 5 business days for production, plus client review window*

What happens: I produce 1 to 3 Stylescape options depending on the clarity of direction captured during onboarding. Each Stylescape encapsulates the intended visual language, UI component style, and tonal direction for the redesign. The client selects one direction before any screen design begins.

Milestone: One Stylescape direction approved by the client.

Client Responsibility: Review the options and provide a clear selection with any directional notes. This is the most important approval gate in the engagement — it protects both sides from scope creep later.

End of Phase: Approved Stylescape locked. Full site redesign begins.

Milestone: Full site prototype complete and ready for review.

Client Responsibility: Stay available for clarifying questions. No heavy lifting required during this phase.

End of Phase: Figma Make prototype link delivered to the designated representative for review.

*Duration: 3 to 5 business days*

Revisions are completed and the updated prototype is resubmitted for final stakeholder approval. A maximum of 2 revision rounds are included — additional rounds beyond that are available as a paid add-on. Once approved, the Webflow build begins.

Milestone: Prototype approved by founder or primary stakeholder.

Client Responsibility: Consolidate all revision requests into a single submission. Ensure the approving stakeholder is available within the review window.

End of Phase: Approved prototype locked. **Second invoice issued (30%). Webflow build begins once payment is confirmed.**

*Duration: 2 to 3 weeks depending on site complexity and add-ons selected*

What happens: The approved designs are built live in Webflow with tokens and variables attached and synced. If the SEO Metadata Setup add-on is selected, title tags, meta descriptions, OG tags, and SEO images are produced and embedded during this phase. If a Platform Adjustment add-on applies, this phase covers the supported alternative platform instead.

Milestone: Webflow site live and matching the approved prototype.

Client Responsibility: Confirm Webflow access and flag any platform-specific constraints before this phase begins.

End of Phase: Live Webflow site delivered. Offboarding document issued. **Final invoice issued (20%). Engagement closed once payment is confirmed.**

## 11. FAQ

### Marketing Website — FAQ

**Fit and Scope**

**Is this service for a new website or an existing one?**
Existing websites only. If you don't have a product yet, branding and foundational work comes before a redesign. UX, Rewritten is specifically for products that are live and need to work better.

**Does this include a full design system?**
No. The Webflow build includes tokens and variables for visual consistency, but a full design system is a separate engagement — see Design System Zero. The two services pair naturally: UX, Rewritten redesigns the experience, and Design System Zero codifies it.

**What if my website is on WordPress, Wix, or Shopify?**
A Platform Adjustment add-on applies. Supported platforms are WordPress with Elementor, Wix, Framer, and Shopify. Any platform outside this list requires a separate conversation during the Discovery Call.

**What if I only want a few pages redesigned?**
UX, Rewritten covers full site redesigns only. Focused work on a specific set of pages for a defined duration is a separate contract arrangement — reach out and we can discuss whether that structure fits your situation.

**Do you write the copy too?**
Yes. Page copy and microcopy are written and embedded directly into the prototype screens. What you see in the prototype is what gets built. Article writing and KB content are explicitly out of scope.

---

**Expected Outcomes and Success Measurement**

**What does success look like at the end of this engagement?**
You walk away with a fully redesigned website, copy written and embedded throughout, and a live Webflow build ready to go. Success is a site that moves users forward intentionally — every page, every flow, and every word designed to guide rather than overwhelm.

**Will this guarantee improved conversion?**
The redesign removes the friction and copy problems that were blocking conversions. Actual conversion improvements depend on implementation, traffic quality, and testing over time. What this engagement delivers is a sound, intentional experience to test from — not a promise of specific metrics.

**How many pages does this cover?**
The full site. There are no artificial page caps — the scope covers every page of your existing website.

Typically 6 to 10 weeks from onboarding to Webflow delivery, depending on site size, complexity, and how quickly your team moves through the approval gates.
