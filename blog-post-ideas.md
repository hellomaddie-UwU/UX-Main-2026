# Blog Post Ideas for hellomaddie.com

Built from a full read of your six case studies, three service pages, site structure, and the "Other Past Works" project content in `index.html`. Every idea below is tied to a specific project, decision, or line from your own copy — nothing generic. Grouped by the four pillars you flagged (Design Systems & Documentation, Solo/Hybrid Designer-Developer Life, CRO & Business Impact, Tutorials), plus a bonus personal-essay group and a new group mining the four smaller "Other Past Works" projects.

Goal alignment: personal brand / hiring signal, thought-leadership, and freelance lead gen. Tone: your case-study voice — blunt, reflective, no spin — dialed up to be more personal.

## 1. Design Systems & Documentation

This is your deepest, most repeated well — three case studies (WoofPay, both MSPbot design system projects) and a service page (Design System Zero) all live here, and they share a spine: every single one ends with "I'd build a website instead of a Figma file." That's not a coincidence, it's a thesis.

1. **"Why I Stopped Building Design Systems in Figma (And Started Building Websites Instead)"** — Pull this straight from the pattern across WoofPay, the MSPbots restructuring, and the Asset Team guidelines. You reached the identical conclusion three separate times, in three separate contexts. That repetition *is* the argument.
2. **"The Design System Nobody Adopted: A Post-Mortem"** — MSPbots restructuring, told straight. "Adoption did not occur" is a stronger hook than any success story you have.
3. **"Design Systems Aren't for Designers — They're for Whoever Has to Use Them Without You in the Room"** — Built around Richard going silent for two months after the WoofPay handoff, and what that silence actually meant.
4. **"I Built a Brand and a Design System From Scratch in One Month. Here's What I'd Cut."**
5. **"Copy-Paste Beats Comprehension: What a Frustrated Front-End Dev Taught Me About Documentation"** — The Glenn/HTML-widget story: you stopped writing instructions and just built the thing.
6. **"The Day I Rejected Atomic Design — and Why I'd Reverse That Decision Now"**
7. **"PM-Facing and Dev-Facing Docs Are Not the Same Document With a Different Font"** — The mid-project audience shift on the MSPbots system, and why it required a rewrite, not an edit.
8. **"What Happens to a Design System When the Team Quietly Switches to ChatGPT Instead"** — This undercurrent runs through both the Asset Team and the restructuring case study. Worth naming directly.
9. **"Do's and Don'ts Beats Design Theory: Writing Guidelines for People Who Aren't Designers"**
10. **"The Design System That Was Built for One Person — and Why That's a Trap"** — Nora's original MSPbots file, and what "personal tool" vs. "shared resource" actually means in practice.
11. **"Consistency Before the Tools Existed: Building WoofPay's System Before Figma Had Variables"**
12. **"How I Turn a Messy, Undocumented Product Into Something an Outsider Can Read"**
