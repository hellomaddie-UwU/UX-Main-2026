# Gallery Image Copy

## Oct_31__2022.png

**Title:** Athena
**Date Finished:** October 31, 2022
**Description:** This piece was inspired by a statue of Athena I came across at MoMA (The Museum of Modern Art), captured in the moment of drawing back her bow. I wanted to put my own spin on that pose, playing with the contrast between her poised, focused expression and the soft, flowing drape of her dress.
**Alt Image Copy:** Digital illustration of the goddess Athena in a white toga, seated while drawing back an ornate bow, wearing a crown and long braided blonde hair.

## Dec_13__2022.png

**Title:** Himeno
**Date Finished:** December 13, 2022
**Description:** Chainsaw Man was airing weekly on Crunchyroll at the time, and after watching the third episode's end credits, I couldn't stop thinking about how striking Himeno looked in that scene. I redrew her pose from it and added the show's title logo just for fun. This one gave me more trouble than expected, mainly because I wasn't used to rendering buildings and cityscapes yet. Looking back at it now, it feels a bit stiff, and it's one I'd like to revisit and redo someday.
**Alt Image Copy:** Fan art illustration of Himeno from Chainsaw Man, sitting on a stool in a dark blue swimsuit against a neon-lit city skyline at night, with the anime's Japanese title logo above her.

## May_20__2023.png

**Title:** Wally Peaking
**Date Finished:** May 18, 2023
**Description:** This one came before the sketch page. I wanted the framing to feel deliberately ambiguous, harmless if you don't know the context, but unsettling once you recognize who's peeking through those red slats. If you're familiar with the source material, you'll know exactly what's going on. If not, it just reads as a curious little muppet looking in from the inside.
**Alt Image Copy:** Close-up fan art of Wally Darling from Welcome Home peeking through red venetian blinds, with only his eyes and hand visible in the gap.

## Jan_1_2024.png

**Title:** Adjusting to the Start of AI as a UX/UI Designer
**Date Finished:** January 1, 2024
**Description:** I made this as the feature image for a Medium post reflecting on what it was like being a UX/UI designer that year. Design workflows were already starting to shift, and AI was still new enough that most people in the industry weren't taking it seriously yet. I wanted the illustration to capture that slightly overwhelmed, coffee-fueled feeling of adjusting to a changing design landscape, complete with a cat draped over the office chair and a UI design tool mockup in the background.
**Alt Image Copy:** Illustration of a tired UX/UI designer resting her head on her hand at a cluttered desk, with a black and white cat lying on the back of her chair, a laptop, coffee mug, and a UI design tool interface in the pink-toned background.
